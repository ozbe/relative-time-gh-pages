Support

Questions, problems, or suggestions about Relative Time? Email relativetime@ozbe.dev and I'll get back to you.

It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

A widget shows the wrong people or places

Press and hold the widget, choose Edit Widget and pick the people and places it shows. Left empty, it follows the first ones in the app's list.

A place I want isn't in the search

When you add someone, the place search looks in a built-in list of cities first, which works offline. When you're online it also searches Apple Maps for smaller places. Try the nearest larger city in the same time zone if you're offline.

To find someone already in your list, use the search button next to the add button instead.

I can't move a row where I want it

Press and hold a row, then drag it up or down. The list is grouped by time zone, so a row moves within its own group. A person's row moves to another group when you change where they are.

I deleted a row by mistake

Shake your iPhone, or swipe left with three fingers, to undo the delete.
