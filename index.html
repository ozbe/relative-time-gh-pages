Relative Time

What time is it for everyone else?

Relative Time is a world clock for iPhone. Add the people in your life and where they are, and see at a glance who's awake, who's at work and who you'd be waking up.

Join the beta on TestFlight
Install TestFlight on your iPhone, then open the link there.

Drag the 24-hour scrubber and every row, and the map, moves to that moment. Rows are grouped by time zone, with real sunrise and sunset times and a day or night mark from the actual position of the sun.

Switch to the world map to see time zones shaded by offset and the day and night line for the time you've scrubbed to. Give a person a photo or emoji, a note and their working hours, and see whether they're available. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets keep your favourite clocks one glance away.

Each time zone heading shows the hours you share with the people in it, so you can find a time that works for everyone. If you've saved a person's number, swipe their row to call them, or press and hold it to call, message or FaceTime. The search button finds anyone in your list by name or place.

Relative Time works with VoiceOver and Dynamic Type, and respects Reduce Motion.

Everything stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no tracking.
